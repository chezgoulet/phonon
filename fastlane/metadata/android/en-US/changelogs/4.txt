Gate B of the audit, plus the rest of Gate A.

Stopping the server now survives a service restart: the operator's intent is
persisted instead of inferred from the boot setting. Switching a model marks a
reload in flight, so health no longer reports ready while the previous model is
still answering. A failing reload gives control endpoints back after two
minutes instead of five.

Model names that resolve to "." or ".." are refused rather than quietly renamed,
and a download can be started by catalog id over the API, which is the path that
carries the hash and the signed record.
