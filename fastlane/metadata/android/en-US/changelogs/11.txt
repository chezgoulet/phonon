Works on phones other than the one it was built on.

The native build now ships one CPU backend per ARMv8 level and lets llama.cpp
pick the best the running phone supports, instead of baking in dot-product
instructions that older phones do not have. The app checks the CPU before launch
and refuses with a message rather than letting the engine die on an illegal
instruction.

Also: Android 10 is supported again (minSdk 29); the server is refused when the
model and its cache will not fit in currently available memory, and an unexpected
exit that looks like a low-memory kill is reported as such; thermal decisions use
the platform's thermal headroom where it exists, with battery temperature as the
fallback; and the Performance card no longer prints Pixel 7 cluster names on
other phones.
