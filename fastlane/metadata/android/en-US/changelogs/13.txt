The app can now measure instead of guessing, and it can say what it found.

'Tune this phone' in Settings runs a short sweep: three decode configurations and
two prefill thread counts through the app's own endpoint, keeps the best of each,
saves a profile keyed by the phone's SoC, and restarts the server with it. The
profile records the llama.cpp revision it was measured against, so an engine
update discards it instead of applying stale tuning. The sweep is manual, takes a
couple of minutes, and never changes the chosen model.

Settings also gains vendor guidance: background-restriction behaviour for the
phone's manufacturer, with a link to the right settings screen where a known
intent exists and a link to dontkillmyapp.com, and a warning when the phone has
been held at 100% on the charger for more than four hours.
