Fixes a regression that broke the workload Phonon exists for: prompts long
enough to take more than two minutes of prefill were disconnected before the
first token. The stall watchdog now times a blocked write rather than a quiet
socket, so a backend that is still thinking is left alone, and the upstream read
timeout matches the gateway's.

Also hardens the proxy: request bodies are read only after the rate limit and the
API key are checked, oversized bodies are refused with 413 instead of buffered,
and the request head has byte and time budgets so a slow client cannot pin a
worker.

Adds tools/proxy-harness, a desktop-JVM regression suite that compiles
ApiServer.kt against stubs and runs it in front of a fake llama-server; it covers
silent prefill, a stalled reader, unauthenticated large bodies, the body cap and
a dripping head. CI runs it when the proxy changes.
