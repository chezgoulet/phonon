A new identity, and three things that make the node easier to live with.

- CORE: a thermal-readout look — hard edges, condensed display type, light and dark
  following the system, the BENCH mark as the logo and the launcher icon.
- The mark is alive: it draws the bus, runs a packet along the traces and turns its
  corners; green means serving, amber means paused on heat, red means a fault.
- Home Assistant: the built-in Ollama integration now finds this node — a conversation
  agent and Assist, with no extra software to install.
- Battery: a charging-limit card that opens the system screen, confirms the state, and
  reports hours spent above 80% this week.
- Gateway: optional one-switch LiteLLM registration with the key encrypted on the device.
- Model downloads: memory pressure now warns with the numbers and lets you continue,
  instead of refusing the download outright.
