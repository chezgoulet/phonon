The model cards now say what they know and where it came from.

Every measured figure names the device it was measured on; a Pixel 7 number shown
on another phone is labelled as a reference rather than as this phone's own
result. Cards carry architecture, licence and OSI status, and what the model is
good at. Vendor claims are marked vendor-reported and sourced — MiniCPM5's
tau2 score is the first — instead of reading like something we measured.

The "multimodal" claim is withdrawn. This build never passes --mmproj, so no
model can accept an image; Gemma 4 and Qwen3.5 are text-only here until that path
is wired.
