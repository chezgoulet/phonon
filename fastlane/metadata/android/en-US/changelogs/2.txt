Fixes from the first round of review.

A battery pause is no longer undone by the thermal resume on the next check, so
a discharging phone stops serving instead of restarting the model every thirty
seconds. Two simultaneous download requests can no longer both start writing the
same file. The in-app benchmark now dials the address the app is actually bound
to, so it works when serving on a specific LAN address rather than a wildcard.
