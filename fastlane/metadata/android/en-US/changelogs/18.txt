The build is now reproducible: two clean checkouts, built at different paths,
produce the same APK byte for byte. That is what lets anyone check that the
binary matches the source, and what F-Droid needs to publish it.

Getting there removed four sources of drift — absolute build paths in the debug
info, the linker build-id, and the engine's browser UI, which was fetched and
gzipped during the build. The engine now ships without that UI; the phone
serves the API the app exposes, and the build no longer needs the network.
